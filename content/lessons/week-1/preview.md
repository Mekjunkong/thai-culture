## A useful first hello

A polite **สวัสดีครับ / ค่ะ** (*sà-wàt-dii khrap / kha*) works for hello and goodbye. Add **ขอบคุณครับ / ค่ะ** (*khàawp-khun khrap / kha*) when you want to say thank you.

## Try it today

1. Say **สวัสดีครับ/ค่ะ** to one service worker.
2. Say **ขอบคุณครับ/ค่ะ** after paying.
3. Notice whether you need the **khrap/kha** ending to sound warm and polite.
